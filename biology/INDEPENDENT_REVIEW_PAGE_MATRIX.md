## 5. Assignment and acceptance cautions

- The reviewer may mark a claim “out of expertise”; this is a referral, not a pass.
- A page may receive G5 approval while its G4 scientific review remains open, or vice versa. Track the gates separately.
- A claim requiring a new source must be checked against the source itself, not accepted because the author has added a citation.
- If a specialist cannot resolve a taxonomic concept, retain the uncertainty and document the authority/version consulted.
- No reviewer should approve the 52 deferred illustrations based on text briefs alone. Each rendered image needs a separate visual scientific review after production.
- Do not use this matrix to claim WCAG, screen-reader or physical-device compliance; those are separate test gates.
