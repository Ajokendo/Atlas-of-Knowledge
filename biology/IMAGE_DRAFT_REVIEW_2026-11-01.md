# Biology image draft review — 1 November 2026

## First production attempt: Batken flora concept

**Status: local draft preview only — not approved and not integrated into a chapter.**

A first visual concept was generated from the Batken flora P1 brief and composed as two separate, unlabeled botanical panels: an Aygul-like fritillary and a representative wild tulip. The source was a generated multi-panel image sheet (ImageGen generation ID `2f3c828d-4528-4352-aff1-251faaf756fe`). The generated captions were removed during cropping; no scientific labels, locality claims or distribution map were retained.

A 1280×720 WebP review master was created in the working session as `/mnt/data/flora-batken-candidate.webp`. It is not yet stored in the repository. It is a concept preview, not a publication asset.

## Scientific review required before approval

1. The chapter uses `Petilium eduardii` for Aygul, while older/common references may use `Fritillaria eduardii`. Final caption and alt text must follow the chapter's accepted nomenclature and note synonymy only where appropriate.
2. The source sheet originally labeled the red tulip `Tulipa greigii`. That label was removed, because the Batken chapter lists `Tulipa rosea`, `T. ferganica` and `T. affinis` in protected-area documentation and does not verify that the pictured morphology is one of those taxa.
3. The right-hand flower is therefore only a generic visual concept. It must not be captioned as a named Batken species unless a botanist confirms the diagnostic morphology and locality.
4. The image is not evidence of the exact Aygul-Tash population, abundance, flowering date or access conditions. Do not imply visitor access or encourage collection/trampling.
