## Common deferral rationale

Several local-only visual concept drafts remain unapproved and are not counted as completed illustrations. Rejected or ambiguous drafts are documented in `IMAGE_DRAFT_REVIEW_2026-11-01.md`. The 23 produced briefs comprise 15 source-backed, locally packaged WebP photographs and 18 original SVG schematics. Regional fauna schematics now include Naryn, Osh, Batken and Talas; the Talas SVG uses Ministry camera-trap evidence from Besh-Tash and keeps aquatic fauna unlabelled where species identification is inconsistent. Other briefs retain their exact insertion markers until approved assets are integrated. No third-party image is accepted without a verified source and licence.

For every deferred item:

- **Source / licence:** no image asset has been selected or used; third-party source, licence and attribution are therefore N/A at this stage. Any future sourced image must have a verified reuse licence and attribution before insertion. Any future original artwork must have its creator and licence recorded.
- **Scientific review:** P1 briefs are reviewed in `IMAGE_SCIENTIFIC_REVIEW.md`; P2/P3 briefs are reviewed individually in `IMAGE_BRIEF_REVIEW.md`. Local concept drafts exist for a subset of subjects, but no draft is accepted for this mapped asset. Before activation, the actual image, species identity, locality, scale, caption and bilingual alt text must be checked.
- **Integration:** deferred briefs retain their original `image-brief-*` anchors. A produced figure preserves its original anchor ID. The central map retains target filenames and composition briefs; the current full-page regression audit still identifies outstanding missing regional anchors.
- **Release treatment:** do not display a blank image, broken image path or unlicensed substitute. Keep the text note visible until an approved asset is integrated.
