## Common deferral rationale

Historical concept drafts and rejected alternatives are not counted as assets. The current 53-row register records an asset or integration for every mapped brief; rejected or ambiguous drafts remain documented in `IMAGE_DRAFT_REVIEW_2026-11-01.md` and are not substitutes for the accepted assets. The produced set includes source-backed, locally packaged WebP photographs and original SVG schematics. Regional fauna schematics include Naryn, Osh, Batken, Talas and a composite national habitat panorama; the Talas SVG uses Ministry camera-trap evidence from Besh-Tash and keeps aquatic fauna unlabelled where species identification is inconsistent. Focused browser visual QA remains outstanding for entries explicitly marked pending. No third-party image is accepted without a verified source and licence.

For every deferred item:

- **Source / licence:** no image asset has been selected or used; third-party source, licence and attribution are therefore N/A at this stage. Any future sourced image must have a verified reuse licence and attribution before insertion. Any future original artwork must have its creator and licence recorded.
- **Scientific review:** P1 briefs are reviewed in `IMAGE_SCIENTIFIC_REVIEW.md`; P2/P3 briefs are reviewed individually in `IMAGE_BRIEF_REVIEW.md`. Local concept drafts exist for a subset of subjects, but no draft is accepted for this mapped asset. Before activation, the actual image, species identity, locality, scale, caption and bilingual alt text must be checked.
- **Integration:** deferred briefs retain their original `image-brief-*` anchors. A produced figure preserves its original anchor ID. The central map retains target filenames and composition briefs; the current full-page regression audit still identifies outstanding missing regional anchors.
- **Release treatment:** do not display a blank image, broken image path or unlicensed substitute. Keep the text note visible until an approved asset is integrated.

The project owner's definitive instruction is that **all external checking, review, assessment, peer review, specialist expertise, certification and expert sign-off are excluded from the Atlas of Knowledge project**. No external person, school teacher, university, laboratory, NGO, company, consultant or other organisation is to be engaged to inspect, approve, certify or evaluate the atlas as part of this project's work. Do not list any external review as a development task, acceptance gate, pending item, blocker or planned project deliverable. Do not create an EXTERNALLY VERIFIED status for project acceptance.

All quality assurance within this project is performed by the project team: editorial review; fact-checking against published primary/authoritative sources; taxonomy, locality, date and numerical consistency checks; transparent uncertainty notes; attribution and rights review; bilingual checks; code, browser, accessibility and visual-rendering tests. These are internal development controls and must be described as such. If such work is later undertaken independently, it belongs to a separate process and separate records. Historical references in this document to pending independent, second-person external or specialist review are superseded by this rule; they must not be interpreted as open project tasks.
