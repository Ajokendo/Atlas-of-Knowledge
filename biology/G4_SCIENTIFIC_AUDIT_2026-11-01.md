## Method

Reviewed regional occurrence and taxonomic claims against their cited primary/official sources and current specialist taxonomic references. Checked whether dates, survey footprints, methods, evidence types and uncertainty were preserved, and whether Russian and English passages express the same scope. Claims were not upgraded beyond what the cited evidence supports.

## Findings and decisions

### Fauna chapter

1. **Common Crane at Jety-Oguz (2026) — supported with appropriate qualification.** The Ministry notice dated 3 June 2026 reports a nest found on 19 April and a pair with two chicks observed on 24 May in the Jety-Oguz section of Issyk-Kul State Nature Reserve. It states that the previous national nesting record was in the Chui Valley in 1958 and that a reserve researcher's paper had been submitted to the Eurasian Crane Working Group bulletin. The chapter accurately attributes the record to the Ministry, distinguishes it from a population trend or range-expansion claim, and does not imply that the submitted paper was already published. No change required. Source: https://mnr.gov.kg/ru/posts/news/pervoe-gnezdovanie-serogo-zuravlya-na-vostocnom-pobereze-ozera-issyk-kul

2. **Bird taxonomy reference — verified.** AviList v2026 was published on 20 October 2026. Its own description identifies it as a coordinated taxonomic and nomenclatural checklist with revised range information; it is not evidence of a local Kyrgyz occurrence or a conservation assessment. The chapter states precisely these limitations. No change required. Source: https://www.avilist.org/checklist/v2026/

3. **Son-Kul fish survey (2023) — source discrepancies correctly retained.** The published article abstract describes field visits in 2021–2023, while its Materials and Methods section lists 2020, 2021 and 2023. The chapter flags this mismatch rather than silently selecting one date range. The article's fish material lists whitefish, peled, naked osman, scaled osman, Tibetan stone loach and lake minnow, while the chapter carefully distinguishes lake fish from the taxa reported in the Kok-Jerty outflow. Further verification of the exact locality assignment and vouchers remains desirable. Source: https://knau-bulletin.com/en/journals/tom-21-3-2023/nauchnyye-isslyedovaniya-v-rybolovstvye-na-primyerye-dvukh-promyslovykh-vidov-ryb-na-ozyerye-son-kul

4. **Suusamyr Valley fish survey (2024; published 2025) — numerical contradiction correctly flagged.** The article abstract reports four recreational-fishing species plus one weed fish; the body and conclusion state four fish species total. Its species table and text use the name `Triplophysa stoliczkai`. The chapter does not present the abstract's “4 + 1” as a settled total and retains the identification as the authors' determination pending diagnostic/voucher review. No unsupported reconciliation was made. Source: https://doi.org/10.36979/1694-500X-2025-25-12-159-166

5. **Fish taxonomy — current catalogue distinction verified.** Eschmeyer's Catalog of Fishes (species record updated 13 August 2026) treats `Triplophysa elegans` as valid and gives Kyrgyzstan in its distribution, while IUCN's assessment for `T. stolickai` uses a different taxonomic concept and its stated range does not include Kyrgyzstan. The chapter explicitly explains the conflict and does not silently merge the concepts. For `Gymnodiptychus dybowskii`, the current Catalog and ITIS treat that combination as valid and `Diptychus dybowskii` as a synonym/earlier combination. The chapter's cross-reference is appropriate. Sources: https://researcharchive.calacademy.org/research/ichthyology/catalog/fishcatget.asp?spid=53209 ; https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2022-1.RLTS.T168488A1186806.en ; https://researcharchive.calacademy.org/research/ichthyology/catalog/fishcatget.asp?spid=55960 ; https://itis.gov/servlet/SingleRpt/SingleRpt?print_version=PRT&search_topic=TSN&search_value=689192&source=to_print

6. **Maral and argali figures — scope safeguards retained.** The regional chapter distinguishes the Naryn Valley extrapolation from national statistics, keeps first-day argali observations separate from a full survey or national total, and preserves the article–Dryad provenance discrepancy for the genomic study. These are appropriate evidence boundaries; no numeric aggregation or inferred trend was introduced.

7. **Tibetan Snowcock and candidate Black-necked Crane records — appropriately cautious.** The chapter distinguishes the Himalayan Snowcock's documented Kyrgyz records from the unconfirmed range-edge claim for Tibetan Snowcock. The candidate Black-necked Crane report is not promoted to the confirmed national list. No change required.

### Flora chapter

8. **National vascular-plant total (4,043) — verified.** Sennikov et al. (2025) explicitly report a revised national checklist total of 4,043 vascular plant species after taxonomic corrections, ten new country records, one confirmed record and two exclusions. The chapter correctly dates the total and does not present it as a count of all organisms or a permanent final figure. Source: https://doi.org/10.1002/njb.04731

9. **Kulun-Ata flora totals — scope distinction retained.** The 2026 literature review reports approximately 394 higher-plant species based on older literature and explicitly warns that some taxonomic names are outdated. The chapter distinguishes this from a separate 2026 university expedition figure of 808 and does not treat either as a single settled, fully reconciled checklist. Source: https://ilim.uia.gov.kg/index.php/main/ru/article/view/917

10. **Aygul / Eduard's fritillary — correction made.** The chapter previously said that the plant is “now assigned to the genus `Petilium`” without stating the competing current taxonomic treatment. Kew's Plants of the World Online / World Checklist of Vascular Plants (2026) accepts `Fritillaria eduardii` and lists `Petilium eduardii` as its synonym. The local Kyrgyz source uses `Petilium eduardii`; to preserve the name used in that regional evidence and maintain continuity with the local source, the chapter retains it as the local-source name but now explicitly cross-references the accepted POWO name in both Russian and English. A direct link to POWO was added. This is a taxonomic clarification, not a change to the documented locality or conservation narrative. Source: https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/535120-1

11. **Besh-Aral, Besh-Tash and regional flora records — evidence scope appropriately qualified.** The chapter distinguishes 2022 plot monitoring published in 2025 from complete reserve checklists, KBA trigger taxa from whole-park inventories, and herbarium-supported records from unsettled taxonomic groups. No unsupported oblast-wide presence claim was identified in this bounded pass.

## Block 2 — taxonomy, systematics and evolution (completed)

**Scope:** `chapters/systematics.html` and `chapters/evolution.html`.

1. **Three-domain classification — qualified, not discarded.** The chapter presented Bacteria, Archaea and Eukarya as the modern system without clearly distinguishing the widely used three-domain classification from alternative phylogenomic tree hypotheses. Current work continues to support eukaryotes as closely related to and potentially nested within Asgard archaea; the precise branching placement is still actively investigated. The chapter now describes the three-domain framework as widely used, explains that some phylogenomic analyses support a two-domain model, and cites Zhang et al. (2025, *Nature*). The Russian and English text, interactive rank explanation, learning objective, quiz wording and explanatory feedback were aligned. This avoids presenting a teaching classification as a settled statement about the deepest branching topology. Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08955-7

2. **Species concepts and speciation — scientifically sound at the intended level.** The chapter explicitly states that the biological species criterion is not applicable to every organism, including bacteria and fossils, and notes hybridisation and asexual reproduction as complications. Its allopatric/sympatric descriptions, polyploidy example and prezygotic/postzygotic barrier distinctions are consistent with OpenStax. Added a direct OpenStax section reference specifically covering new-species formation and reproductive barriers. Source: https://openstax.org/books/biology/pages/18-2-formation-of-new-species

3. **Cambrian boundary — corrected to the current chart.** The interactive timeline used the older rounded value 541 Ma. The International Commission on Stratigraphy 2026/06 chart gives the Cambrian base as 538.8 ± 0.6 Ma. Updated the Russian and English descriptions and the visible button label, including the language-switch label map, so the outdated 541 Ma value no longer reappears after switching languages. Source: https://stratigraphy.org/chart/

4. **Earliest evidence of life — uncertainty made explicit.** The former 3.8 Ga description said only “possible early evidence”. It now explains that reported geochemical and morphological biosignatures in 3.8–3.7 Ga Isua rocks are disputed because of strong metamorphism, while more robust and widely accepted evidence is known from about 3.5 Ga. Both language versions of the default display and interactive timeline now match. Source: https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2024.0106

5. **Other timeline anchors — checked and retained at appropriate precision.** The Great Oxidation Event at about 2.4 Ga is consistent with the evolutionary chronology literature; early `Homo sapiens` fossils at about 300 ka are supported by Jebel Irhoud finds. The dinosaurs-at-about-230-Ma entry is retained as a rounded educational marker, not an exact first-occurrence date. Sources: https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.1603076 ; https://humanorigins.si.edu/research/whats-hot-human-origins/our-species-arose-least-300000-years-ago

6. **Evolutionary mechanisms and tree-reading concepts — no material error found in this pass.** The descriptions of mutation, natural selection, genetic drift, gene flow, homology, analogy, clades and phylogenetic trees maintain the necessary distinctions. The beetle selection model is labelled illustrative rather than predictive and correctly notes that response depends on heritability.

## Block 3 — cell biology, genetics, microbiology and biochemistry (completed)

**Scope:** `chapters/cell-biology.html`, `chapters/genetics.html`, `chapters/microbiology.html` and `chapters/biochemistry.html`.

1. **Cell biology — core concepts and mechanisms checked.** The cell-theory summary, prokaryote/eukaryote distinction, organelle functions, passive and active membrane transport, osmosis, cell-cycle phases, G1/S and G2/M controls, spindle checkpoint, mitosis, cytokinesis, apoptosis and cell signalling are consistent with standard teaching references. The cell chapter correctly separates interphase from mitosis and treats prometaphase as a staging convention rather than forcing one universal phase scheme. The English telophase output omitted the Russian sentence that cytokinesis usually divides the cytoplasm; this bilingual scientific-content mismatch is now aligned. Sources: https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/4-3-eukaryotic-cells ; https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/5-2-passive-transport ; https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/10-2-the-cell-cycle

2. **Genetics — inheritance and gene expression checked.** The chapter correctly qualifies the simple Mendelian model (complete dominance, diploid loci, probabilistic ratios), distinguishes genotype and phenotype, and notes polygenic/environmental influences. Transcription, translation, RNA processing, codons, start/stop signals, functional non-coding RNAs, genetic-code exceptions, mutation classes, gene regulation and epigenetic inheritance are presented with appropriate caveats. The DNA-to-RNA-to-protein plate identifies reverse transcription as an exception and does not imply that every gene encodes a protein.
   - **Coverage gap corrected:** the chapter listed DNA replication among its key topics and diagrammed DNA → DNA, but did not provide a substantive explanation. Added a bilingual learning block covering semiconservative replication, origins, helicase, primase, 5′→3′ DNA polymerase activity, leading and lagging strands, Okazaki fragments and ligase. The text distinguishes many bacterial chromosomes with a single origin from eukaryotic nuclear chromosomes with multiple origins. A direct OpenStax reference was added. Source: https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/14-chapter-summary

3. **Microbiology — cellular organisation and microbial mechanisms checked.** The chapter distinguishes Bacteria and Archaea from acellular viruses; its treatment of bacterial shapes, binary fission, viral replication, lytic/lysogenic bacteriophage cycles, closed-culture growth phases, antibiotic resistance and the three major horizontal gene-transfer routes (transformation, transduction and conjugation) is scientifically appropriate at the intended level.
   - **Overgeneralisation corrected:** “bacteria are single-celled” is now “typically single-celled”, with a short note on filaments, biofilms and differentiated colonies. A 2025 review is cited because bacterial multicellularity is diverse and its definition remains an active research topic. The new wording avoids suggesting that all bacterial populations are simply isolated single cells. Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11948493/

4. **Biochemistry — energy and enzyme descriptions checked.** The enzyme model correctly distinguishes activation energy from reaction equilibrium; the temperature, pH and substrate-concentration explanations are appropriately qualitative. The ATP section distinguishes standard biochemical free-energy change from actual cellular ΔG. The Russian interactive ATP-hydrolysis output omitted the explicit concentration dependence already present in English; it has been corrected to match. Glycolysis is correctly described as yielding net 2 ATP and NADH per glucose; the eukaryotic citric-acid-cycle location and mitochondrial electron-transport-chain location are correctly distinguished, and oxygen is identified as the terminal electron acceptor in aerobic respiration.

5. **Bilingual and interaction checks.** Russian/English content was checked for the corrected passages. The cell-cycle telophase description, DNA-replication block, bacterial-cell qualification and ATP ΔG explanation now carry matching scientific meaning in both languages.
