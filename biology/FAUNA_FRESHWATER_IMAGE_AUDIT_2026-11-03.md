## Evidence checked in the chapter

The freshwater section distinguishes inland-water habitat types and explicitly explains that fish communities differ among drainage basins. It does not support a single undifferentiated national fish assemblage.

The Issyk-Kul paragraph reports the bounded FAO-supported survey of 19–24 August 2024: three sampling sites near Toru-Aygyr, Chok-Tal and Cholpon-Ata; 72 specimens; 55 Issyk-Kul chebak (`Leuciscus schmidti`), 10 whitefish (`Coregonus lavaretus`) and 7 pikeperch. This is a dated sample, not a complete lake checklist.

The chapter also cites the historical FAO 2013 synthesis (73 species: 49 native and 24 introduced) and an incomplete FishBase country checklist checked on 1 November 2026 (31 species currently present). These figures have different scope and must not be combined or depicted as a definitive current total.

The 2026 Institute of Biology / American Museum of Natural History research covered the Issyk-Kul, Chu and Talas basins; preliminary identifications were reported, but laboratory processing and analysis were still continuing. These materials are not a published final national checklist.

## Locked visual composition

1. **Lake panel — Issyk-Kul:** show a recognisable large alpine lake, a gently sloping stony/sandy littoral zone and clear underwater water column. Fish may be shown as naturalistic, unlabelled schematic forms. Do not attach species labels to individual fish unless their morphology and taxonomic identity are independently checked against a specialist reference.
2. **Mountain-stream panel:** show a separate cold, fast-flowing Tien Shan stream with coarse substrate and shallow riffles. Do not imply that the three fish taxa sampled in the 2024 Issyk-Kul survey also inhabit this stream. Do not add named fish taxa without a basin-specific chapter record.
3. **Ecological details:** a few aquatic-invertebrate forms may be included as schematic food-web context, without species names. Do not add amphibians unless the depicted breeding habitat and taxon are supported by the chapter.
4. **Habitat separation:** lake and stream must be visibly distinct scenes or clearly separated panels. Avoid a single composite water body that suggests direct hydrological connection between Issyk-Kul and a mountain river.
5. **No unsupported totals:** no species counts, endemicity claims, distribution maps, flags, labels or conservation-status symbols.
6. **Accessibility:** provide concise Russian and English alt text and captions. State that fish forms are illustrative and not a species-level identification plate.

## Taxonomic cautions carried forward

- Do not print a national fish-species total on the image.
