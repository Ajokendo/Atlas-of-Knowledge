### Shamshy-Tuyuk

Official Ministry notice, 22 September 2025:
https://mnr.gov.kg/en/posts/news/v-zapovednike-samsy-tuyuk-ustanovleny-fotolovuski-dlya-izuceniya-sneznogo-barsa

The notice reports installation of 27 camera traps to study snow leopard presence and habitat. It documents research activity, not by itself a confirmed image of a snow leopard.

A separate 2026 scientific study by Kinoshita et al. concerns camera-trap evidence of snow leopard–Myricaria interactions in Shamshy. The article and its Dryad dataset should be used for scientific context; the Dryad package is a spreadsheet and README, not a video archive.

Zenodo record previously identified as the associated video deposit:
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19751807

The record lists six MP4 files and CC BY 4.0. The actual video binaries have not been downloaded or individually inspected in this working environment. Do not claim that a particular file contains a usable snow-leopard frame until it has been downloaded and viewed. For high-resolution source material, the record directs users to contact the rights holders. No contact has been sent from this workflow.
