## Review of all mapped briefs

| Image anchor | Priority | Scientific review / constraints |
|---|---:|---|
| `image-brief-biology-cover` | P2 | Use an abstract educational composition; do not imply a literal evolutionary ladder or depict DNA/cell structures with fabricated molecular geometry. No unverified species labels. |
| `image-brief-anatomy-systems` | P2 | Keep organs in anatomically plausible positions and proportions; distinguish organ systems without duplicating or omitting structures. Avoid decorative organs not present in human anatomy. |
| `image-brief-anatomy-circulation` | P2 | Show four heart chambers and valves coherently; pulmonary and systemic circuits must be continuous and correctly directed. Red/blue is a conventional oxygenation key, not literal blood colour. |
| `image-brief-anatomy-homeostasis` | P3 | Represent negative feedback as receptor → control centre → effector → response reducing the original deviation. Do not depict homeostasis as a one-way growth chain. |
| `image-brief-biochem-molecules` | P3 | Distinguish carbohydrates, lipids, proteins and nucleic acids. Lipids are not conventional repeating-unit polymers; do not depict amino acids, nucleotides or monosaccharides as interchangeable building blocks. |
| `image-brief-biochem-enzyme` | P2 | Show substrate binding at an active site and product release without implying the enzyme is consumed. Avoid a rigid lock-and-key metaphor as a universal mechanism. |
| `image-brief-biochem-respiration` | P2 | Place glycolysis in cytosol, the citric-acid cycle in mitochondrial matrix and the electron-transport chain on the inner mitochondrial membrane. ATP synthase orientation and proton gradient must be coherent. |

| `image-brief-botany-flower` | P3 | Use a generic angiosperm flower unless a species is explicitly named. Distinguish anther, stigma, style, ovary and ovules; do not imply every flower has identical symmetry or structure. |
| `image-brief-cell-animal` | P3 | Do not add a cell wall or chloroplast to an animal cell. Organelles should be recognisable but not shown at mutually misleading scale. |

| `image-brief-cell-mitosis` | P2 | Show chromosome condensation, spindle attachment and separation in the correct order. Keep mitosis distinct from interphase and cytokinesis; do not duplicate chromatids after separation. |

| `image-brief-evolution-homology` | P2 | Homologous structures indicate shared ancestry even when functions differ; analogous structures may share function without the same structural origin. Do not conflate the two. |
| `image-brief-evolution-timeline` | P2 | Use a proportionate geological timescale and checked dates. Avoid a linear procession from “primitive” to “advanced” organisms or false simultaneity. |

| `image-brief-genetics-translation` | P2 | Show mRNA read 5′→3′, codon/anticodon pairing, tRNA delivery and peptide-bond formation in the ribosome. Do not reverse the reading direction or confuse transcription with translation. |
| `image-brief-genetics-mutations` | P2 | Distinguish substitution, insertion and deletion; frameshifts generally follow non-triplet insertions/deletions. Do not imply that every mutation is harmful or changes phenotype. |
| `image-brief-microbe-compare` | P3 | A bacterium is a cell with prokaryotic organisation; a virion is acellular. Viral envelopes are not universal, and bacterial structures such as peptidoglycan must not be assigned to all microbes. |

## Cross-cutting production rules

1. Do not add scientific labels, exact localities, range boundaries, abundance figures or conservation categories that are not supported by the relevant chapter.
2. Keep diagrams internally consistent: correct direction, sequence, structures, scale conventions and scientific terminology.
3. Use a composite-scene caption when different habitats or localities are combined; do not imply all species coexist at one point or season.
4. Distinguish a simplified educational model from a literal scale drawing. State simplifications where they could otherwise mislead.
5. Review Russian and English alt text and captions independently for equivalent meaning.
6. No image may be marked produced until the exact target file exists, its provenance/licence is recorded, and scientific/editorial review is complete.
