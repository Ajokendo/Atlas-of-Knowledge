## Cross-cutting production rules

1. Do not add scientific labels, exact localities, range boundaries, abundance figures or conservation categories that are not supported by the relevant chapter.
2. Keep diagrams internally consistent: correct direction, sequence, structures, scale conventions and scientific terminology.
3. Use a composite-scene caption when different habitats or localities are combined; do not imply all species coexist at one point or season.
4. Distinguish a simplified educational model from a literal scale drawing. State simplifications where they could otherwise mislead.
5. Review Russian and English alt text and captions independently for equivalent meaning.
6. No image may be marked produced until the exact target file exists, its provenance/licence is recorded, and scientific/editorial review is complete.
