# Chapter 02 — visual replacement plan

**Working branch:** `workspace/geography`  
**Scope:** the 11 local Chapter 02 SVGs whose rights records state **NO PUBLIC LICENCE ASSERTED / NOT CLEARED FOR REUSE**.  
**Policy:** IMAGE-FIRST POLICY v1.2.  
**Review date:** 5 November 2026.  
**Purpose:** identify source assets with a documented educational fit and item-level reuse terms before replacing the unlicensed SVGs. This plan is not itself a licence grant and does not approve any unlicensed local SVG for publication.

## Decision rules

- A candidate is not approved merely because it appears on an official or educational website.
- Record the exact asset, creator/source, publication or version date where available, exact licence or usage statement, attribution, and any jurisdiction or adaptation limits.
- Where the source has multiple credited components, verify those components individually before reuse.
- A visual may be split into a small, coherent panel set if one image cannot accurately cover the required concept.
- Do not use an image whose subject is only superficially similar to the required learning objective.
- Keep bilingual captions and alt text. Do not describe a model, map or diagram as quantitative when it is conceptual.

## Replacement priority

1. **First implementation batch:** 2-A, 2.2-A, 2.5-A, 2.9-A, 2.12-A and 2.14-A are integrated. 2.8-A remains conditional until the exact image file can be retrieved and its delivery verified.
2. **Second implementation batch:** The first replacement batch is now integrated; continue with 2.6-A and 2-D rights closure.
3. **Rights closure before implementation:** 2.6-A and 2-D. Verify the credited components and exact OpenStax image terms; do not rely only on the book-level licence.
4. **Continue targeted discovery:** 2.1-A, 2.2-A and 2.11-A. Do not force an unsuitable image into these slots.

## Important distinction

The status **READY FOR REPLACEMENT** means the source and its stated reuse terms are sufficiently documented to proceed to the visual-integration and technical checks. It does not mean that the image has already been downloaded, embedded, visually inspected in the chapter, or passed browser/accessibility QA.

The 5 remaining local SVGs are **NOT CLEARED FOR PUBLIC REUSE** until each is removed from public-facing use or a verified rights holder separately grants permission. The former 2-A, 2.2-A, 2.5-A, 2.9-A, 2.12-A and 2.14-A local SVGs were removed after their sourced replacements were integrated. This plan does not grant a licence to those files and does not extend the Chapter 01 CC BY-NC 4.0 confirmation to Chapter 02.
